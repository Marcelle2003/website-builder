# Website brief

The AI creates and maintains these notes from conversation and project evidence; the owner does not need to fill out this document. Keep it short and current, preserve owner edits, and replace these prompts with actual information. Separate confirmed decisions, agent assumptions, and open questions. Do not store secrets or duplicate the conversation.

## Purpose and visitors

Business/site purpose, intended visitors, and their primary action.

## Scope and behaviour

Requested pages and features, roles if applicable, existing behaviour to preserve, and work deliberately deferred.

## Creative direction

Brand name, tone, references, colour roles, typography, image treatment, motion preferences, and decisions the owner likes.

## Content and editing

Supplied versus demonstration content, imagery sources/credits, Wagtail-editable fields or sections, and the publish-to-website update path.

## Project and preview

Stack and selected versions, where run instructions live, local preview/CMS URLs, and relevant environment-variable names without values.

Keep detailed architecture and verified commands in `AGENTS.md` and the run guide; reference them here instead of maintaining competing copies.

Potential improvements to the reusable Website Builder workflow belong in local `SKILL_FEEDBACK.md`, following the skill's feedback guidance. Keep this brief focused on the website; do not put private inbox paths here.

## Latest work and verification

Completed changes, actual checks/evidence, known limitations, and the next requested step.
